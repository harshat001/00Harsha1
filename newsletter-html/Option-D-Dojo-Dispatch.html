Policy Change

Your PF Just Went Up. We’re Covering Our Side of It.

The government raised the PF wage ceiling from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000, effective Sept 2026. That means more money goes into your PF every month, yours and ours.

Normally the employer’s share of that increase would come out of your CTC. We decided it shouldn’t.

ContentNinja is absorbing that extra cost by bumping up your CTC, so your gross salary stays exactly the same. Your take-home dips a little since your own PF share goes up too, but that’s just extra money heading straight into your retirement, nowhere else.

Full breakup in Keka. Questions?
